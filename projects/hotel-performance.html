Hotel performance Power BI dashboard

A business intelligence dashboard analyzing hotel sales, bookings, and customer ratings across regions and booking channels.

Role
Dashboard developer
Status
Completed
Tool
Power BI

Overview

A Power BI dashboard built to analyze hotel business performance — sales, bookings, average daily rate (ADR), and customer satisfaction — across hotels, regions, and booking channels.

Objective

Turn raw booking and sales data into a single reporting view that surfaces which hotels, regions, and channels are performing well, and where customer ratings need attention.

Approach

I used Power Query to clean and shape the underlying data, then built the data model and calculated measures in DAX. The report layout was designed around the KPIs that matter most for hotel operations — total sales, total bookings, average ADR, and average customer rating — with drill-down views by hotel, region, month, customer segment, and booking channel.

What the dashboard covers

  • Total sales and total bookings at a glance
  • Average ADR and average customer rating as headline KPIs
  • Sales broken down by hotel, region, and booking channel
  • Monthly sales trend over time
  • Sales by customer segment
  • Average customer rating by hotel

Dashboard Preview

The final Power BI dashboard brings together financial performance, booking activity, pricing, customer satisfaction, hotel performance, regional sales, customer segments, and booking channels in one reporting view.

Power BI Hotel performance dashboard overview

What I learned

This project strengthened my practical understanding of the BI workflow, from exploring and preparing transactional data to selecting appropriate visualizations and building an interactive Power BI dashboard. I worked with Power Query for data preparation and quality checks, then used business-focused visualizations to analyze financial performance, bookings, customer segments, booking channels, regional performance, sales trends, and customer satisfaction.
